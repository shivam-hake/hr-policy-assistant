# Work from Home and Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Hybrid work

### 1.1 Eligibility
Confirmed employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, as agreed with their reporting manager. Employees on probation work from the office on all working days unless their manager approves otherwise in writing.

### 1.2 Office days
Each team agrees its common office days with its manager. Employees are expected to be in the office on those days.

### 1.3 Core hours
All employees, whether at home or in the office, must be available between 11:00 and 16:00 IST on working days.

## 2. Full-time remote work

### 2.1 Approval
Working fully remotely requires written approval from the department vice president and HR. Approvals are reviewed every 6 months.

### 2.2 Working from another city
Employees may work from a city other than their base location for up to 15 working days per calendar year, with manager approval. Travel and stay for this are at the employee's own cost.

### 2.3 Working from abroad
Working from outside India is not allowed without prior written approval from HR and the legal team, because of tax and data-protection rules.

## 3. Equipment and costs

### 3.1 Equipment
Kestrel provides a laptop and headset. Employees working from home at least 1 day per week may claim a one-time home office allowance of INR 5,000 for a chair or desk, once every 3 years.

### 3.2 Internet reimbursement
Employees with an approved hybrid or remote arrangement may claim up to INR 1,000 per month for home internet. The claim is made through the expense portal with the monthly bill attached.

### 3.3 Security when working remotely
Employees must use the company VPN on home networks and must not work on confidential data over public Wi-Fi. See the IT and Device Use Policy (IT-2).
