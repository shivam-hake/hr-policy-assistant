# Code of Conduct

## 1. Conflicts of interest

### 1.1 Declaring conflicts
Employees must declare in the HR portal any situation where personal interests could conflict with Kestrel's, such as a close relative working at a vendor or a competitor.

### 1.2 Outside work
Paid work outside Kestrel, including freelancing, needs prior written approval from HR. Work for a competitor or a client is not allowed.

## 2. Gifts

### 2.1 Gift limit
Employees may accept gifts from vendors, clients or partners worth up to INR 2,000 per person per year. Gifts above this value must be declined or handed to HR. Cash or vouchers are never accepted, whatever the amount.

## 3. Respect at work

### 3.1 Harassment
Kestrel does not tolerate harassment, discrimination or bullying of any kind.

### 3.2 Sexual harassment complaints
Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee under the POSH Act. Complaints can be made by email to ic@kestrel.example within 3 months of the incident and are handled confidentially.

## 4. Speaking up

### 4.1 Whistleblowing
Concerns about fraud, bribery or other serious misconduct can be raised with the Ethics Committee at ethics@kestrel.example or through the anonymous ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a disciplinary offence.

## 5. Confidentiality and public communication

### 5.1 Confidential information
Employees must not share confidential company, client or employee information outside Kestrel, during or after employment.

### 5.2 Social media
Employees may mention that they work at Kestrel on social media, but must not post confidential information or speak on behalf of the company unless authorised by the communications team.

## 6. Breaches

### 6.1 Disciplinary process
Breaches of this code are investigated by HR. Depending on severity, outcomes range from a written warning to termination of employment.
