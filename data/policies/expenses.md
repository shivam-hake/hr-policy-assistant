# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. General rules

### 1.1 Submitting claims
Expenses are claimed in the expense portal within 30 days of being incurred, except travel expenses, which follow the Business Travel Policy (TRAVEL-5.1).

### 1.2 Receipts
Original receipts or invoices are required for every expense above INR 500. Expenses of INR 500 or less can be claimed without a receipt, up to INR 2,000 per month in total.

### 1.3 Approval
Claims up to INR 25,000 are approved by the reporting manager. Claims above INR 25,000 also need approval from the department head.

### 1.4 Payment
Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.

## 2. Regular allowances

### 2.1 Mobile phone
Employees in customer-facing, sales and on-call roles may claim up to INR 800 per month for their mobile phone bill.

### 2.2 Home internet
Home internet is reimbursed only under the Work from Home Policy (WFH-3.2), not under this policy.

## 3. Learning and development

### 3.1 Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of INR 25,000 per financial year for courses, books, conferences and certification exams related to their role. Spending needs manager approval before enrolling.

### 3.2 Certification bonus
Employees who pass an approved professional certification also receive a one-time bonus of INR 10,000, paid after the certificate is uploaded to the HR portal.

### 3.3 Repayment if leaving
If an employee leaves within 12 months of a reimbursed course or certification costing more than INR 15,000, the reimbursed amount is recovered in the full and final settlement.

## 4. Team expenses

### 4.1 Team meals
Managers may spend up to INR 1,000 per team member per quarter on team lunches or dinners. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## 5. Not reimbursed

### 5.1 Excluded expenses
The following are never reimbursed: alcohol, traffic fines and penalties, personal entertainment, gym memberships, laundry for trips shorter than 5 days, and expenses for family members.
