# IT and Device Use Policy

## 1. Company devices

### 1.1 Laptops
Each employee receives a company laptop, which is replaced every 4 years or earlier if faulty. Company laptops are for work use; limited personal use is allowed.

### 1.2 Software
Only software from the company software catalogue may be installed. Other software needs approval through an IT service ticket.

### 1.3 Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen company device must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours, so the device can be locked and wiped remotely.

## 2. Security

### 2.1 Passwords and sign-in
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and must not be reused from personal accounts.

### 2.2 VPN
The company VPN must be used for all work outside the office network.

### 2.3 Phishing
Suspicious emails must be reported with the "Report phishing" button in the mail client. Employees who clicked a suspicious link must also inform the IT service desk immediately.

## 3. Personal devices

### 3.1 Using personal phones
Company email and chat may be used on a personal phone only after it is enrolled in the company's mobile device management (MDM) app.

### 3.2 Storing company data
Company data must not be stored on personal laptops, personal USB drives or personal cloud storage.
