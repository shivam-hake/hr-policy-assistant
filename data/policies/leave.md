# Leave Policy

## 1. Scope

### 1.1 Who this applies to
This policy applies to all full-time employees of Kestrel Technologies Pvt. Ltd. in India. Interns and contract staff follow the leave terms in their own agreements.

### 1.2 Leave year
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Leave balances are shown in the HR portal.

## 2. Casual and sick leave

### 2.1 Casual leave
Employees receive 8 days of casual leave per leave year, credited on 1 January. New joiners receive casual leave on a pro-rata basis for the remaining months of the year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

### 2.2 Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per leave year. Unused sick leave lapses at the end of the year and cannot be encashed.

### 2.3 Medical certificate
A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive working days. It must be uploaded to the HR portal within 3 working days of returning to work.

### 2.4 Leave during probation
During the probation period, employees may take casual leave and sick leave only. Earned leave accrues during probation but can be used only after confirmation.

## 3. Earned leave

### 3.1 Accrual
Earned leave accrues at 1.5 days per completed month of service, up to 18 days per leave year.

### 3.2 Carry forward
Up to 30 days of unused earned leave can be carried forward to the next leave year. Any balance above 30 days lapses on 31 December.

### 3.3 Applying for earned leave
Earned leave must be applied for in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance and approved by the reporting manager. Earned leave of more than 10 consecutive working days also needs approval from the department head.

### 3.4 Encashment
Earned leave is not encashed while employed. On exit, it is encashed as described in the Exit and Notice Period Policy (EXIT-4.2).

## 4. Parental and other leave

### 4.1 Maternity leave
Eligible employees receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for each of their first two children, and 12 weeks for a third child. Up to 8 weeks can be taken before the expected delivery date.

### 4.2 Paternity leave
Employees receive 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.

### 4.3 Bereavement leave
Employees receive up to 3 working days of paid leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent-in-law).

### 4.4 Leave without pay
If all applicable leave is exhausted, an employee may request leave without pay of up to 30 days in a leave year. It needs approval from both the reporting manager and HR.

## 5. Public holidays

### 5.1 Holiday calendar
Kestrel observes 10 fixed public holidays and 2 optional holidays each year. The calendar for each office is published in the HR portal in December for the following year.
