# Business Travel Policy

## 1. Before travelling

### 1.1 Approval
All business travel must be approved by the reporting manager in the travel portal before booking. International travel also needs approval from the department head.

### 1.2 Booking
Flights, trains and hotels are booked through the company travel desk. Bookings made directly by employees are reimbursed only if the travel desk could not book them, and the employee must state the reason in the claim.

### 1.3 Travel advance
Employees may request a travel advance of up to 75% of the estimated trip cost. The advance must be settled in the expense claim for the trip.

## 2. Travel class

### 2.1 Flights
Domestic flights are booked in economy class. International flights longer than 6 hours may be booked in business class for employees at grade M3 and above; all other international flights are economy.

### 2.2 Trains
Train travel is booked in AC 2-tier, or AC first class for grade M2 and above.

## 3. Accommodation

### 3.1 Hotel limits
Hotels are booked by the travel desk within these nightly limits: INR 7,000 in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) and INR 4,500 in other cities.

### 3.2 Staying with friends or family
Employees who stay with friends or family instead of a hotel receive a fixed allowance of INR 1,000 per night.

## 4. Daily allowance

### 4.1 Domestic per diem
A per diem covers meals and local incidentals: INR 2,500 per day in metro cities and INR 1,800 per day in other cities. Receipts are not required for the per diem.

### 4.2 International per diem
The international per diem is USD 75 per day, or the local equivalent.

### 4.3 Local transport
Taxis and app-based cabs between the airport, hotel and work locations are reimbursed at actual cost with receipts.

## 5. Claims

### 5.1 Claim deadline
Travel expense claims must be submitted within 15 days of returning from the trip. Claims submitted later need department head approval.
